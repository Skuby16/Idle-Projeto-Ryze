# Primeira etapa de arte — Vila de Ryze

As cinco imagens desta pasta foram criadas do zero com a ferramenta integrada de geração de imagens. A imagem conceitual enviada pelo usuário guiou a paleta, a iluminação e a composição; ela não foi copiada para o jogo. Cada personagem e o portal são arquivos com transparência e podem ser substituídos por animações depois.

| Arquivo | Uso no jogo |
| --- | --- |
| `village-courtyard.png` | Chão e arquitetura da Vila; centro livre para caminhada |
| `swordsman.png` | Espadachim na Vila e no Mundo dos Esqueletos |
| `merchant.png` | Mercador, perto da loja à esquerda |
| `portal-commander.png` | Comandante, acima e à esquerda do portal |
| `skeleton-portal.png` | Vórtice mágico sobre o arco de pedra |

## Prompts finais

Todos os prompts foram executados com a ferramenta integrada, sem API ou serviço adicional. O fundo foi pedido como imagem opaca; os outros quatro arquivos foram pedidos com transparência real.

### Cenário

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: background illustration for a playable 2D isometric fantasy RPG scene in Phaser. Create an ORIGINAL environment illustration, no source screenshot replication. Isometric view, wide 3:2 composition. A medieval stone village courtyard at dusk, dark stylized fantasy with richly textured cobblestones. Leave the central and lower-middle courtyard broad, flat, unobstructed and visually clear for a player character to walk across. On the LEFT side, a timber-and-stone merchant shop with warm amber lantern light, awning, crates and barrels. Across the UPPER background, walls and distant roofs. On the UPPER RIGHT, a large ancient stone archway with a DARK EMPTY opening prepared for a separate animated magical portal overlay; a small guard post nearby. Cool blue dusk shadows, restrained warm torchlight, restrained teal accents. Readable game environment, hand-painted 2D illustration, strong isometric geometry, detailed but not cluttered. Perspective should support a hero walking left-right across the central stone floor. No people, no characters, no skeletons, no UI, no icons, no signs, no writing, no logo, no watermarks. Architecture should stay mainly in the top and far sides; avoid any foreground objects blocking the bottom middle. Avoid photorealism and avoid direct copying of the supplied concept image.

### Espadachim

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: single 2D character sprite for a Phaser isometric RPG. Create an ORIGINAL full-body Espadachim (swordsman) hero, standing in an isometric three-quarter view facing slightly toward the viewer and right. Adult fantasy adventurer in dark steel and leather armor, deep red short cape, one readable silver sword held low, sturdy boots. Human proportions, clear silhouette at small size. Rich hand-painted 2D game illustration with restrained painterly texture, matching a dark fantasy stone village at dusk, warm amber edge light and cool blue shadows. Center the complete figure with generous transparent margin, feet visible, no ground or cast shadow (the game supplies shadow), no other characters or objects. No text, logo, border, UI, watermark, sheet grid, or scenery. Designed for a 100px-tall game sprite. Genuine transparent alpha background.
